## 1. Introduction
SVGViewer quickly shows where SVG files live on your drive and previews them.

## 2. Getting started
Launch the application and pick a drive at the top to begin.

## 3. Choosing a drive
Use the drive dropdown in the top-left.

## 4. Navigating folders
Click through the tree view to open folders.

## 5. Recognizing folders with SVGs
Folders containing SVGs are marked. Set the filter to "SVG only" to see just
those folders.

## 6. Previews and preview size
Pick a size at the top-right: Large, Medium, Small, or Only details.

## 7. Editing an SVG
Double-click a preview to open the file in the associated editor.

## 8. Switching language
Choose your language in the toolbar (NL/EN/DE). The UI updates instantly.

## 9. Opening help
Click the Help button to open this guide in your language.
